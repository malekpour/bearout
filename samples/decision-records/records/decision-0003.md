# Review cadence for open proposals

## Context

Proposals that nobody revisits rot.
[decision-0001-ruling-01](decision-0001.md#decision-0001-ruling-01) makes
acceptance permanent, so a proposal should be revisited on a schedule.

## Options

- Review every open proposal monthly.
- Review on demand when a dependent record is opened.
