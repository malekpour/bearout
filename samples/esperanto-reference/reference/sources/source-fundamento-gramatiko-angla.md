# Fundamento de Esperanto, Grammar (English)

## Summary

The English text of the Fundamento's grammar: the alphabet (section A), the
parts of speech (section B, rules 1 to 8), and the general rules (section C,
rules 9 to 16). Locators are rule numbers.
