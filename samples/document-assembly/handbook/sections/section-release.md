# Releases

## Text

A [release](../glossaries/glossary-core.md#glossary-core-term-release) of
{Project} is cut by a
[maintainer](../glossaries/glossary-core.md#glossary-core-term-maintainer)
from a tagged commit whose
[changelog](../glossaries/glossary-core.md#glossary-core-term-changelog)
entry is complete.
