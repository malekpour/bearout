# Review

## Text

Every change to {Project} receives a
[review](../glossaries/glossary-core.md#glossary-core-term-review) by one
other contributor, and a change to a release branch is also approved by a
[maintainer](../glossaries/glossary-core.md#glossary-core-term-maintainer)
before it merges.
