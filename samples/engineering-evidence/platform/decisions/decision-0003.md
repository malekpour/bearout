# Bulk transfers share the cyclic channel

## Context

Sharing one undifferentiated channel makes the worst-case cyclic timing a
function of whatever maintenance activity is running. Rejected in favour of
the service classes in
[decision-0001-ruling-02](decision-0001.md#decision-0001-ruling-02).
